# NIMT Admission Counsellor Incentive Guide

**Effective Date:** July 2026 | **Version:** 2.0

---

## How Your Incentive Is Calculated

Every admission you close earns you an incentive. The final amount depends on four things:

1. **Which course** the student enrolled in
2. **How the lead reached us** (the source)
3. **Whether you scheduled and confirmed a campus visit**
4. **How many total admissions you close in the month** (your achievement multiplier)

Your projected earnings update live on your dashboard — check it anytime.

## 2. Lead Source Percentage

Your incentive is adjusted based on how the lead was sourced. Higher effort = higher reward.

| Lead Source | You Get |
|------------|---------|
| Self-generated (referral, personal networking, alumni) | 100% of base |
| Institutional outreach (school visits, education fairs) | 90% of base |
| Digital (website, Google/Meta ads, Shiksha, CollegeDunia, JustDial, WhatsApp, etc.) | 80% of base |
| Walk-in (no prior scheduled visit) | 50% of base |
| Consultant | No incentive |

### What counts as a walk-in vs. a scheduled visit?

This is important — it directly affects your payout:

- **Full incentive (per source %):** You scheduled a campus visit **at least 2 hours before** the visit time, the student showed up, and you completed the visit in the system.
- **Walk-in rate (50%):** The student walked in without you having scheduled a visit, OR the visit was auto-scheduled by the system (Navya), OR you scheduled it less than 2 hours before.

**Example:** A lead came from Google Ads (digital = 80%). If you scheduled their visit yesterday for today and they came → you earn 80%. If they just walked in without a scheduled visit → you earn 50%, even though they came from Google Ads.

**Tip:** Always schedule the visit in the CRM when you confirm it with the student. This is the single biggest thing you can do to protect your incentive.

## 3. Monthly Achievement Multiplier

At the end of the month, your base incentives are multiplied based on how well you hit your targets. Achievement is the **lower** of your admission % and revenue %.

| Achievement | Multiplier |
|-------------|-----------|
| Below 70% | No incentive for the month |
| 70–89% | 0.75× |
| 90–99% | 1.00× |
| 100–109% | 1.25× |
| 110–124% | 1.50× |
| 125–149% | 1.75× |
| 150%+ | 2.00× |

The multiplier applies only to the base course incentive. Speed bonus, token bonus, and volume bonus are paid as-is.

### Your Monthly Targets

| Designation | Admission Target | Revenue Target |
|-------------|-----------------|---------------|
| Junior Counsellor | 10 | ₹8,00,000 |
| Counsellor | 12 | ₹10,00,000 |
| Senior Counsellor | 15 | ₹13,00,000 |
| Team Leader | As assigned |

**Important:** B.Sc Nursing and B.Ed Kotputli admissions pay ₹0 base incentive but they DO count toward your admission and revenue targets. Don't ignore these leads — they feed your multiplier.

## 4. Bonus Incentives

### Token Bonus
- **₹100** per admission token collected
- One token bonus per lead (collecting a second token on the same lead doesn't earn extra)
- Paid with the monthly statement, not weekly
- If the token is refunded, the bonus is recovered

### Speed Conversion Bonus
- Converted within **48 hours** of lead creation → **₹500**
- Converted within **7 days** → **₹250**
- Beyond 7 days → No speed bonus
- Not available on consultant leads

### Monthly Volume Bonus
Only the highest slab you reach is paid:

| Admissions in Month | Bonus |
|---------------------|-------|
| 5 | ₹1,000 |
| 10 | ₹2,500 |
| 15 | ₹5,000 |
| 20 | ₹10,000 |
| 30+ | ₹20,000 |

### Team Bonus
When your **entire campus** hits its target, every eligible counsellor gets:

| Campus Achievement | Bonus per Counsellor |
|-------------------|---------------------|
| 100% | ₹3,000 |
| 120% | ₹7,500 |
| 150% | ₹15,000 |

You must be individually eligible (see below) to receive the team bonus.

## 5. Eligibility — What Can Disqualify You

You must meet **all** of these to receive any incentive for the month:

| Gate | Requirement |
|------|------------|
| Admission target | ≥ 70% achieved |
| Revenue target | ≥ 70% achieved |
| Attendance | ≥ 95% (entered by HR) |
| No disciplinary action | No misconduct during the month |

**Daily KPIs** (fresh calls, follow-ups, WhatsApp messages, visits, applications) are tracked and visible on your dashboard and your monthly statement, but they are **advisory** — they won't block your incentive. They help your admission head understand your work pattern.

If you're ineligible, your statement will show exactly which gate you failed and the gross amount you would have earned — so you always know what's at stake.

## 6. Worked Examples

### Example 1 — MBA, Self-Generated, 125% Achievement
- Course: MBA → ₹3,000 base
- Source: Self-generated → 100%
- Scheduled visit completed → full rate
- Base = ₹3,000 × 100% = ₹3,000
- Monthly achievement: 125% → 1.75× multiplier
- Incentive = ₹3,000 × 1.75 = **₹5,250**
- Add: ₹500 speed bonus (converted in 48h) + ₹100 token = **₹5,850 total**

### Example 2 — BBA, Digital Lead, Scheduled Visit, 110% Achievement
- Course: BBA → ₹2,000 base
- Source: Digital → 80% (scheduled visit exists)
- Base = ₹2,000 × 80% = ₹1,600
- Achievement: 110% → 1.50×
- Incentive = ₹1,600 × 1.50 = **₹2,400**
- Add: ₹250 speed bonus + ₹100 token = **₹2,750 total**

### Example 3 — BBA, Digital Lead, Walk-in (No Scheduled Visit), 110% Achievement
- Same as Example 2, but the student walked in without a scheduled visit
- Source capped at 50% (walk-in treatment)
- Base = ₹2,000 × 50% = ₹1,000
- Incentive = ₹1,000 × 1.50 = **₹1,500**
- Difference from Example 2: **₹900 less** — just because no visit was scheduled

### Example 4 — BA LL.B, Walk-in, 95% Achievement
- Course: BA LL.B → ₹2,500 base
- Walk-in → 50%
- Base = ₹2,500 × 50% = ₹1,250
- Achievement: 95% → 1.00×
- Incentive = **₹1,250** + ₹100 token = **₹1,350 total**

### Example 5 — B.Sc Nursing
- No incentive payable regardless of source or performance
- But the admission counts toward your target → helps your multiplier on other courses

### Example 6 — Student Withdraws
- You earned ₹5,850 on an MBA admission
- Student withdraws with a fee refund within 45 days
- **Entire ₹5,850 is recovered** from your next incentive payment
- After 45 days, the incentive is safe

## 8. Visit Center — Your Daily Tool

The **Visit Center** is where you manage visitors:
- **Record Walk-in** button (also in the top navbar — use it from any page)
- **On Campus** tab — see who's currently checked in, elapsed time, and quick actions:
  - Send a payment link
  - Create a fee proposal
  - Record a token payment
  - Check the visitor out when they leave
- **Today & Upcoming** — check visitors in, mark no-shows, complete visits
- **Post-Visit Queue** — follow up after visits

### Check-in / Check-out Rules
- When a visitor arrives, **check them in** (or record a walk-in, which auto-checks-in)
- When they leave, **check them out**
- If you forget: the system auto-checks them out when you start calling another lead, or at 6 PM daily
- While a visitor is checked in, you'll be redirected to the Visit Center if you try to open another lead's page — check out your visitor first

## 9. Important Rules

### Lead Ownership
- The lead belongs to whoever is assigned in the CRM
- Lead source **cannot be changed** after creation (only an Admission Head can change it)
- All changes are permanently recorded

### CRM Compliance
You must record every interaction in the CRM:
- Every call and its outcome
- Every WhatsApp message
- Document uploads
- Visit status updates
- Counselling remarks

Failure to maintain CRM may result in rejection of incentives.

### What Will Forfeit Your Incentive
- Fake or duplicate admissions
- Fraudulent documentation
- Manipulating CRM records
- False lead ownership claims
- Concealing cancellations
- Misconduct with students or parents

---

## 10. Payment Timeline

1. Admission happens → qualifying fee is realized → incentive **accrues** (you can see it on your dashboard)
2. Month ends → system computes your achievement, multiplier, eligibility → **statement generated**
3. Admission Head reviews and **approves** the statement
4. Accounts processes **payout** with next month's salary
5. If a student withdraws within 45 days → incentive is **recovered** from your next payout
